## Conservative display policy

Use the signed raw logits and mean direction. Positive or negative labels require
at least 5/6 frame signs to agree; otherwise abstain. Fewer than three distinct
frames abstains. This adapts the pre-existing consistency concept to this model,
and was examined on these results: it is not independently calibrated/validated.
Positive label is 'AI-like visual signal'; negative is 'No strong AI evidence',
with an explicit warning that generated content can be missed. No percentage or
'real' verdict is emitted. Raw logits remain inspectable in details.

- Motion set: 4 AI flagged, 1 AI missed; 5 real negative, 1 real inconclusive.
- Earlier nine-image set: 2 AI flagged, 1 AI missed, 1 AI inconclusive;
  5 real negative. The safeguard reduces recall versus the raw authors' rule.
- Older image pair: 1 AI flagged, 1 real negative.

This is a provisional local experimental upgrade, not a declaration of reliable
production accuracy. Keep animation, compression, mixed and edited content in
future testing. Never count inconclusive AI as successful detection.
